About these figures

These are provider-listed estimates for the regular standby line, not single rider or Express Pass. "Closed" is the provider's operational status; "Unknown" means a current value could not be verified. Colors represent the length of the wait, not a whole-park crowd score. Some provider updates may arrive late.

    Weather sources and wind note

    JMA observations are from Osaka Observatory, not from inside USJ. The hourly forecast near the park is supplied by MET Norway. An average wind speed of 7 m/s or more calls for caution with outdoor rides; 10 m/s or more calls for stronger caution. These are this site's guidance, not USJ operating rules or a prediction of closure.
